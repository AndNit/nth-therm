## Bogie Hearth Furnaces IWO – Professional Solution for Heavy Loads

The **IWO bogie hearth furnace** from NTH Therm is the preferred solution for heat treatment of large and heavy workpieces. Thanks to the drive-out bogie, batches can be loaded easily by forklift or crane – without crane movements inside the furnace.

### Why the IWO Is the Right Choice

- **Maximum loading flexibility** – the bogie drives out, the batch is placed on it, the bogie returns
- **Uniform temperature distribution** through forced air circulation (< ±5 °C)
- **Robust frame construction** in powder-coated steel, stainless steel or heat-resistant material depending on temperature range
- **Custom dimensions** – from compact units to large-scale systems for loads of several tonnes

### Technical Equipment (Standard Version)

**Housing & Mechanics**
- Robust powder-coated steel frame construction
- Manually operated bogie (electric drive optional)
- Floor-mounted rails, optionally flush-integrated
- Side-opening door

**Heating & Insulation**
- Heating elements in double walls
- Forced air circulation for uniform temperature distribution
- Chamber material depending on temperature: steel (<300 °C) / stainless steel (<500 °C) / heat-resistant (<860 °C)
- Door seal: silicone (<260 °C) or high-temperature fibre (360–860 °C)

**Control & Safety**
- Touch-screen user interface
- PID controller, Type K thermocouple
- Semiconductor relay (SCR)
- Independent over-temperature protection
- Supply and exhaust air dampers
