## Chamber Furnace ICO – Precise Temperature Distribution for Demanding Processes

The **ICO chamber furnace** from NTH Therm is the ideal all-rounder for heat treatment processes up to 860 °C. The integrated recirculation fan ensures excellent temperature uniformity of less than ±5 °C, a prerequisite for reproducible results.

### Technical Equipment

- Forced air circulation, vertical or horizontal
- Chamber material depending on temperature range
- Door seal silicone (<260 °C) or high-temperature fibre (>260 °C)
- Touch-screen control, PID controller, Type K thermocouple
- Supply/exhaust dampers, over-temperature protection

### Options

Brick-lined chamber · SiC floor tiles · Heating element protection · Cooling fan · Inert gas connection · Electric door lift · Touch screen · Rotary table · Signal lights · Zone control · RS232/RS485 · PC software
