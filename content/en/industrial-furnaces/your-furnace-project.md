## Your Furnace Project – A Custom Quote in 5 Steps

Answer the following questions as accurately as possible – the more we know, the more precise our quote can be. All information is voluntary; an incomplete enquiry is welcome too.

### 1. What needs to be heated?
Describe the material or component to be treated (e.g. steel shafts, aluminium castings, ceramic samples).

### 2. Sizing
- Maximum component size (L × W × H in mm)
- Maximum batch weight (kg)
- Desired useful chamber volume (optional)

### 3. Process Parameters
- Maximum process temperature (°C)
- Typical process temperature (°C)
- Atmosphere: Air / Inert gas (N₂, Ar) / Vacuum / Protective gas / Other
- Is a solvent used? (Yes/No)

### 4. Loading & Automation
- Loading method: manual / forklift / crane / roller conveyor / other
- Desired degree of automation

### 5. Delivery Date
By when does the furnace need to be delivered at the latest?
