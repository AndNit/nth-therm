## Tube Furnace TH1 – Precision for Lab and Production

The **TH1 tube furnace** is designed for continuous operation at maximum temperature and is at home in both laboratory and production environments. Double ceramic tubes, wire heating elements between the tubes, and a compact stainless-steel housing make it robust and long-lasting.

**Particular strengths:** Vacuum connection to 10⁻⁶ mbar · Quartz tube systems · Inert gas operation · Tube rotation system · Insulation plugs · Radiation shields
