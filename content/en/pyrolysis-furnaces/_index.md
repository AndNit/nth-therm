## Pyrolysis Furnaces – Clean Through Thermal Decomposition

NTH Therm distributes the pyrolysis furnaces of **Pyrox bv** (Netherlands, founded 2012) in Germany. Pyrolysis is the most economical method for thermal cleaning of metal components, faster than sandblasting, safer than solvent cleaning, without residues.

### Application Areas

**Coating lines:** Regular cleaning of paint hooks, hanging racks, frames and trays in powder coating and wet paint systems. References: Robberechts NV (Turnhout, Belgium), ABL-Technic Uden BV (Netherlands).

**Food industry:** Thermal cleaning of baking trays, grids and baking moulds, without chemicals, without residues.

**Heat exchangers:** Removal of fouling and oxide layers from heat exchangers.

**Motor rewinding:** Thermal removal of insulating varnish and synthetic resins from motor windings, prerequisite for rewinding.
