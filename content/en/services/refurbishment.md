## Refurbishment and Modernisation – More Performance from Existing Systems

Industrial furnaces have a long service life – but controls, safety systems and insulation age. NTH Therm modernises your existing furnace to current standards: new PLC, touch screen, safety relays, energy optimisation.

### What We Modernise

**Preventive maintenance of industrial electronics:**
- Functional test and visual inspection (panel, cables, terminals)
- Replacement of worn hardware
- Update of wiring diagrams
- Spare-parts and repair service

**DGUV-3 safety inspection under DIN VDE 0100-600:**
- Visual inspection (basic protection, protective conductor)
- Electrical measurement (protective conductor current, insulation resistance, loop resistance, touch current)
- Functional test (RCD, motor protection, emergency-stop chain, voltage drop, phase rotation measurement)
