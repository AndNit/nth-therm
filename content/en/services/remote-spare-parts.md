## Remote PLC Maintenance & Spare Parts – Maximum Availability for Your System

The fastest fault resolution is one that requires no travel. With **remote PLC maintenance via secured VPN**, our technicians can analyse your furnace control system directly and in many cases resolve faults without an on-site visit – within minutes rather than hours.

Combined with **structured spare-parts stocking** – stock items from our warehouse, third-party parts through our European supplier network – we keep your downtime to a minimum.

### Remote PLC Maintenance in Detail

**What NTH Therm delivers via remote diagnostics:**
- Reading and analysing the complete PLC fault log
- Online diagnostics of running programmes and status of all I/Os
- Parameter adjustments and recipe optimisation
- Controlled safety overrides for fault analysis (fully logged)
- Software updates and programme corrections
- Remote support for your maintenance staff during mechanical fault resolution

**Response times:**
| Priority | Target |
|----------|--------|
| Production stoppage | First remote diagnosis ≤ 4 hours |
| Restricted operation | First remote diagnosis ≤ 8 hours |
| Planning enquiry | Response ≤ next working day |

**Technical prerequisites:**
- Internet access at installation site (min. 2 Mbit/s, LAN or mobile router)
- Remote maintenance router (installed during commissioning or retrofitted)
- Customer authorisation per session (no permanent access)

### System Monitoring with OEE Software

For customers with the highest availability requirements we offer integration of **Evocon OEE software**: continuous monitoring of system availability, batch times and process parameters – with alerts on deviations, before a stoppage occurs. Analytics run in the browser with no local installation.

The combination of Evocon monitoring and NTH Therm remote maintenance creates a **predictive maintenance architecture**: deviations are detected before they cause failures.

### Spare Parts Supply

**Stock for NTH Therm systems:**
- Thermocouples (Type K, N, S, B) – common sizes in stock
- Solid-state relays (Crydom, Carlo Gavazzi) – all common power classes
- Power contactors and motor circuit-breakers (Siemens, ABB)
- Pilz safety relays and door safety switches
- Light curtains and proximity switches
- Pressure transmitters and pressure switches (atmosphere furnaces)

**Third-party furnace spare parts:**
For furnaces from other manufacturers we source replacement parts through our supplier network in Germany, Austria and the Netherlands. Frequently requested items (standard thermocouples, solid-state relays, standard contactors) are typically sourced within 1–2 working days.

**Spare-parts documentation:**
On request we maintain a documented spare-parts catalogue for your system – including part numbers, suppliers and minimum stock levels. This serves as the basis for your own parts stocking and ensures smooth handover in the event of staff changes.
