## Environmental Simulation – Why Reliable Test Technology Is Critical

Every product that must perform in the real world is exposed to real environmental conditions: temperature extremes, humidity, temperature fluctuations, thermal shocks. Whether an automotive control unit, a medical device, an aerospace component or industrial electronics — behaviour under these conditions determines approval, safety and marketability.

Climatic test chambers create reproducible, standards-compliant test conditions in the laboratory. What takes years in the field is compressed into hours and days in accelerated testing.

NTH Therm, as an authorised partner of **ACS/Angelantoni Test Technologies**, delivers the complete system portfolio of the world market leader — combined with its own installation, calibration and maintenance service.

#### Temperature & Climatic Chambers (Standard)
The core range for dynamic temperature/humidity programmes. Designed for all common standards: IEC 60068, LV124, ISO 16750. Heating and cooling rates up to 5 K/min standard, with optional additional cooling up to 10 K/min. Volumes from 300 to 1000 litres and beyond.

#### Constant Climate Chambers
For long-term conditioning at constant temperature and humidity conditions: storage tests, lifetime tests, shelf-life studies. High-precision control, low energy consumption.

#### Environmental Stress Screening (ESS)
Combined temperature/vibration testing for accelerated failure detection. ESS chambers are equipped for shaker tables and external vibration exciters. Used in series production for early failure detection (infant mortality).

#### Temperature Shock Chambers
For rapid thermal transitions between hot and cold zones. Switchover times under 10 seconds. Critical for components with different coefficients of thermal expansion (PCBs, hybrid modules, solder joints).

#### Walk-In Large-Volume Chambers
For testing complete vehicles, large assemblies or system integration tests. Individually configured — from 5 m³ to over 50 m³. Available with treadmill, shaker platform or drivetrain test stand on request.

### Application Areas by Industry

**Automotive:** Control units, sensors, wiring harnesses, power electronics, battery systems. Standards: LV124, LV148, ISO 16750, AEC-Q100/Q101.

**Aerospace:** Avionics, actuators, structural components. Standards: MIL-STD-810, DO-160, RTCA.

**Electronics & Semiconductors:** Component qualification, ESS in series production, burn-in for power electronics. Standards: IEC 60068, JEDEC.

**Medical Technology:** Implants, diagnostic equipment, sensors. Standards: IEC 60601, ISO 10993.

**Battery Technology & E-Mobility:** Cell characterisation, module conditioning, system ageing. Standards: IEC 62660, UN 38.3.

**Research & Development:** Materials characterisation, materials testing, component development across all sectors.
