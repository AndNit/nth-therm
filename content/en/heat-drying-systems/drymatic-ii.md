## Drymatic II – Silent Intelligence for Fast Drying

The **Drymatic II** is the heart of the Drymatic system. It supplies all adapters and drying mats with controlled heat and independently regulates energy input, airflow and temperature for optimum drying.

### Features

- Silent, intelligent, energy-efficient
- Three operating modes: Positive / Negative / Balanced
- Heat and air exchange in one device
- Class-4 specialist drying capability
- Flexible adapters for cavity drying
- Stackable, roto-moulded double-wall housing
- **28 kg**, portable, standard plug (13 A)
- Touch-safe heater outlet
- Quick-fix hose connector
- Integrated wheels
- Max. airflow: 595 m³/h
