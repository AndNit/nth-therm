## Drymatic Heat Drying Systems – The Next Generation

**Drymatic** uses targeted heat to radically accelerate drying after water damage. Instead of weeks, drying takes only days – with demonstrably better results for floors, walls and building fabric.

**How it works:**
Targeted heat increases the vapour pressure inside wet materials and accelerates evaporation. The humid air is exhausted, replaced by dry outdoor air, and the process restarts – until the material is dry.
