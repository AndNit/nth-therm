## Why Heat Drying Is the Better Method

Conventional construction dryers work by convection: they extract moisture from the room air. The problem: the actual transport of moisture out of the material — out of the wood, the screed, the plaster — happens slowly and in an uncontrolled manner. The machine waits for the material to evaporate on its own.

**Heat drying works differently.** Targeted heat raises the vapour pressure inside the wet material. Moisture is actively driven out of the material structure, absorbed by the warm air and exhausted to the outside. The process is active, controllable and significantly faster.

Results in practice:
- Floors and wall linings are preserved that would otherwise have to be removed
- Drying times are halved in many cases
- Secondary damage from prolonged moisture (mould, shrinkage) is reduced
- The overall claim — and its settlement — becomes smaller

The **Drymatic II** is the centre of the system. It supplies all connected adapters and drying mats with precisely regulated heat, automatically controlling energy input, airflow and temperature.

| Operating modes | Positive / Negative / Balanced |
| Class 4 drying | Yes |
| Construction | Stackable, double-wall roto-moulded housing |

**Three operating modes for every situation:**

**Positive (pressurised mode):** Dry warm air is actively pushed into cavities and structures. Optimal for ventilated hollow floors and wall assemblies accessible from one side.

**Negative (vacuum mode):** Humid air is extracted from cavities and replaced by dry outdoor air. Ideal when air injection is not possible but a suction opening is available.

**Balanced (combined mode):** Simultaneous injection and extraction for complex, enclosed cavity structures. Maximum efficiency for hard-to-access assemblies.

### Boost Bar – Underfloor Drying Without Removal

The **Drymatic Boost Bar** is a flat heating element positioned along skirting boards and under floor coverings. It heats the air layer under hardwood floors, parquet and laminate — without drilling, without removal.

**Applications:**
- Parquet and hardwood floors over concrete decks
- Laminate on dry screed
- Timber-frame floors in older buildings
- Underfloor heating systems with timber structure

The Boost Bar operates in combination with the Drymatic II. Multiple Boost Bars can connect to one Drymatic II, enabling even heating of large areas.

---

### Drying Mats – Controlled Surface Drying

**Drymatic drying mats** enable direct heat transfer to floors and walls. The heavy-duty, water-resistant mats are placed directly on the wet surface and distribute the Drymatic II's heat evenly across the entire contact area.

Advantages over air drying:
- Direct heat transfer without losses into room air
- Controlled temperature at the material surface
- Particularly effective for screed, concrete floors and tiles on wet screed
- Multiple sizes — combinable for large areas

### Application Areas at a Glance

**Residential – Water Damage After Pipe Burst or Leak**
The most common application. Floors and walls can often be fully preserved, significantly reducing the claim for owners and insurers.

**Commercial Property – Rapid Return to Usability**
In commercial buildings, downtime costs money. Heat drying minimises restoration time and accelerates return to operational readiness.

**Screed Drying in New Construction**
Cement and anhydrite screed must reach a threshold moisture level before floor coverings can be laid. Heat accelerates this process by weeks and shortens construction time.

**Listed Buildings and Historic Structures**
Controlled, gentle heat enables the drying of historic building fabric without aggressive mechanical intervention.
